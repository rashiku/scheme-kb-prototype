GOVERNMENT OF EXAMPLESTAN
Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare
Notification No. AGR/2026/114 dated 12 August 2026

Mukhyamantri Kisan Sahay Yojana (MKSY)

The Government of Examplestan has approved a scheme to support small and marginal farmer families during the crop season.

[Page 2]
Benefit
Eligible farmer families will receive financial assistance of Rs. 6,000 per year, paid in three equal instalments directly into the beneficiary's bank account.

Eligibility
1. The applicant must be a permanent resident of Examplestan.
2. The landholding of the family must not exceed 2 hectares.
3. The annual family income must be below Rs. 2,50,000.

Documents Required
1. Aadhaar card
2. Land record (7/12 extract)
3. Bank passbook
4. Income certificate

[Page 3]
How to Apply
Farmers should register on the state agriculture portal, fill in the online form, upload the documents listed above and submit it to the Block Agriculture Officer for verification.

Last date for submission: 31 December 2026.
