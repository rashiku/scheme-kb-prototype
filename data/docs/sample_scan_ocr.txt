GOVERNMENT OF EXAMPLESTAN
Department of Labour and Employment

SHRAMIK AWAS SAHAYATA YOJANA

Benefit
Registered construction workers will get a one-time grant of Rs. 1,20,OOO for building a house.

Eligibility
1. Worker must be registered with the State Construction Workers Board
2. Worker must have completed at least 1 year of registration
3. Worker must not own a pucca house

Documents Required
1. Aadhaar card
2. Board registration card
3. Land ownership proof

How to Apply
Submit the form at the District Labour Office.
